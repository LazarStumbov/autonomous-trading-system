01 WHY OPTIONS — THE LEVERAGE MATH
The core concept: With small capital, buying shares gives you linear returns. A $500 position in RTX stock that moves +8% nets you $40. The same $500 in RTX call options — if structured correctly — can net you $400–$1,500+ on the same 8% move. Options are the legal, regulated lever that transforms small capital into meaningful gains in a fast-moving geopolitical event. The tradeoff: if the trade goes the wrong way, you can lose the entire premium you paid. That's why sizing and discipline are everything.

Manage the Trade — The Rules of Engagement

Check your positions once per hour maximum. Watching every minute causes emotional decisions. Set price alerts through your brokerage app for: (a) each position up 50%, (b) each position down 40%, (c) any major news headlines (ceasefire, Strait of Hormuz update).

If any position is up 100%+ on Day 1: Sell HALF your contracts immediately. Bank the profit. Let the remaining half ride for the continuation move. This "house money" approach locks in real gains while maintaining upside exposure.

If any position is down 40–50%: Cut it. This is your stop loss. Options decay fast and a bad position can go to zero. Do not hope and hold — exit and redeploy the remaining capital later if conditions improve.

Do NOT sell everything at once: Scale out in thirds. Sell 1/3 at 100% gain, 1/3 at 200% gain, hold final 1/3 for the biggest potential move.

07 THE 8 GOLDEN RULES OF THIS TRADE
01
Never Use Market Orders on Options
Always use limit orders. Market orders on options can result in fills 30–50% worse than the midpoint. In a gap-up war market, this is especially dangerous. Set your limit at the midpoint of bid/ask and adjust incrementally.
02
Don't Chase The Open
The first 30 minutes on Monday will see the highest IV and widest spreads of any point in this trade. The RTX premium at 9:32 AM will be 20–40% more expensive than at 10:15 AM. Patience costs you nothing. Impatience costs real money.
03
The Ceasefire Is Your #1 Enemy
Every single position you hold collapses on a credible ceasefire announcement. Have Reuters and Bloomberg alerts active at ALL times. The moment Oman confirms talks, your exit window is measured in minutes, not hours.
04
Take Half Off at 100%
This rule has saved more small traders than any other. When an option doubles, sell half. You've now recovered your entire initial investment and are playing with house money on the rest. Greed at 100% gains has destroyed countless war-trade winners.
05
Stop Loss Is Non-Negotiable
If any position is down 40–50%, close it. No "hoping." Options can go from 50% down to zero in hours. A 50% loss on $350 is $175. A zero loss is $350. Cut fast, cut clean. You can always re-enter. You cannot recover a zeroed-out option.
06
Buy Time — Not Just Price
Choose Mar 21 or Apr 17 expiry, not weeklies. Weekly options expiring Mar 7 are a gamble. If the war takes 8 days to play out properly, a weekly option expires worthless. Spending slightly more for the March 21 or April expiry gives your thesis time to be right.
07
Keep 10% Cash Reserve
Don't deploy 100% of your capital at once. Keep 10% in cash. This cash is your "reload" — if you get stopped out of one position and conditions remain favorable (no ceasefire), you can re-enter at a better price. Dry powder is optionality.
08
This Is A Sprint, Not A Marathon
Geopolitical option trades have a natural shelf life of 5–14 days. After 2 weeks, the market has priced in the "new normal" and the explosive volatility premium decays. Plan to be fully out of all positions within 10–14 trading days of your entry, with or without ceasefire.
