The critical risk: OPEC+ met Sunday and agreed to raise output by 206,000 barrels per day from April — a modest increase representing less than 0.2% of global demand. This is not enough to offset Hormuz disruption and will barely cap the spike. Watch it as a negative catalyst only if the Strait reopens simultaneously.
