Comprehensive weekly performance review. Run the full self-improvement cycle:

1. **Performance Metrics**: Calculate Sharpe, Sortino, win rate, profit factor, expectancy, max drawdown
2. **Strategy Breakdown**: Performance by strategy (momentum, mean reversion, news, copy trade)
3. **Trade Post-Mortems**: Analyze each trade — was entry/exit optimal? Were stops appropriate?
4. **Signal Attribution**: Which signal types were most predictive? Update weights.
5. **Account Review**: Performance of followed traders and PM accounts. Promote/demote.
6. **Parameter Suggestions**: Based on data, suggest strategy parameter adjustments
7. **Market Regime**: Assess current regime (trending, ranging, volatile) and strategy fit
8. **Polymarket Review**: PM bet outcomes, edge accuracy, account tracking performance
9. **Generate Report**: Create weekly PDF with all metrics and save to `data/reports/weekly/`
10. **Send Summary**: Telegram notification with key metrics and recommendations

Present recommendations clearly:
- What to keep doing (working strategies)
- What to change (underperforming parameters)
- What to watch (emerging patterns, upcoming events)
